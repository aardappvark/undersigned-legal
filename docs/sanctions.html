Sanctions & Restricted Regions

Last updated: 8 September 2026

NOTICE: Undersigned is not available for download, installation, or use in the sanctioned and restricted regions listed below. If you are located in, or are a resident or national of, any restricted region, you must not use Undersigned.

1. Compliance Overview

ADappvark is committed to full compliance with applicable international sanctions regimes, export control laws, and trade restriction regulations. Undersigned is geo-blocked and unavailable in all sanctioned and restricted regions identified below.

This policy is maintained in compliance with, among others:

  • Australian Autonomous Sanctions (Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade)
  • United Nations Security Council sanctions
  • United States Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions
  • European Union restrictive measures

2. Restricted Regions

Undersigned blocks access from the following 17 countries. This table is the complete and exact list enforced by the App's on-device region check — there are no additional blocked regions, and none of these 17 is omitted:

The check is performed on your device from your SIM, mobile network and locale country code. It identifies a country, so it cannot single out a sub-national territory: the occupied Ukrainian regions of Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk are not blocked by the App, because blocking them would require blocking the whole of Ukraine. If your use case requires that, it is a policy decision for the publisher and the list in the App must be changed first.

2A. Wallet Screening at Sign-In

In addition to the on-device geographic check above, Undersigned screens the public address of any wallet you connect against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list, checked on-device first, and against TRM Labs' public sanctions-screening service where reachable. This screening runs before the App accepts a wallet, and again before any review, revocation, or reply transaction. A listed address cannot use the App. See the Terms, section 8.

3. User Representations

By downloading, installing, or using Undersigned, you represent and warrant that:

  • You are not located in, under the control of, or a national or resident of any of the restricted regions listed above
  • You are not identified on any applicable sanctions or restricted party lists, including the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List (SDN List), the Australian Consolidated List, or equivalent lists maintained by other jurisdictions
  • You will not use Undersigned in any manner that would result in a violation of any applicable sanctions laws, export control regulations, or trade restrictions
  • You will not provide access to Undersigned to any person or entity located in a restricted region or listed on any applicable sanctions list

4. Enforcement

ADappvark reserves the right to take any action it deems necessary to comply with applicable sanctions and export control laws, including but not limited to:

  • Blocking access to the App from restricted regions
  • Terminating the licence of any user found to be in violation of this policy
  • Cooperating with relevant government authorities in connection with sanctions compliance
  • Updating the list of restricted regions as sanctions regimes change

5. Changes to Restricted Regions

The list of restricted regions is subject to change as international sanctions regimes evolve. ADappvark will update this page to reflect any changes. It is your responsibility to review this page periodically and ensure that your use of Undersigned remains in compliance with applicable sanctions and trade restrictions.

Violations of international sanctions laws can result in severe civil and criminal penalties, including substantial fines and imprisonment. ADappvark is not responsible for any legal consequences arising from your use of the App in violation of applicable sanctions or trade restrictions.
